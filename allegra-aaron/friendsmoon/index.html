The Friendsmoon
Allegra & Aaron · Costa Rica · January 3–7, 2027

We're getting married — and before the big day, we're stealing four nights in Costa Rica with our favorite people. Two volcanoes, a turquoise river, hot springs, jungle adventures, and a whole lot of time together. Here's everything you need to know to come celebrate with us.

01 — The Invitation

Most people do a bachelor and bachelorette party. We'd rather bring our closest friends and family somewhere unforgettable instead. So we're hosting a Friendsmoon — four nights in Costa Rica, the week of our wedding, before everyone heads to the coast for the big day. Think adventure by day, long dinners by night, and the whole group in one place. This page lays out the plan, the two places we'll stay, and exactly what it costs.

04 — Where We Stay · Nights 3–4
Auralta — luxury tented camp by Río Celeste
RÍO CELESTE · TENORIO VOLCANO
Auralta Río Celeste
A luxury tented camp on a private rainforest reserve, beside the famous turquoise river.
The vibe
Glamping done at a five-star level — wild, serene, completely immersed in nature
The setting
14-hectare native forest, 700m of private Río Celeste riverfront, volcano views
On site
Restaurant, bar, infinity pool, spa & massage
Nearby
10 min to the Río Celeste waterfall & Tenorio National Park
Why we love it
  • A total change of pace — the calm, wild counterpoint to Arenal
  • Tents with forest-and-volcano views, en-suite baths, and (on some) private jacuzzis
  • The whole camp is ours — just our group, the rainforest, and the river

Good to know — the camp is gloriously remote, so all meals here are at the hotel restaurant (and they're wonderful). It's all included in your package below.
